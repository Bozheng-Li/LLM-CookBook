The model reads a sequence of tokens and predicts the next one. That single
objective, repeated over enough text, is enough to produce a system that can
summarize, translate, answer questions and write code. Nothing in the training
objective mentions any of those tasks.

A tokenizer sits between raw text and the model. It decides what the atomic
units are. If the tokenizer is bad, the model wastes capacity learning to
reassemble fragments that should have been single units, and every downstream
number — context length, cost per request, throughput — gets worse.

Byte pair encoding starts from raw bytes and repeatedly merges the most frequent
adjacent pair. The merges are learned from the corpus, so a tokenizer trained on
English code will produce different units than one trained on Chinese prose.
This is why vocabulary choice is a data decision, not just an engineering one.

注意力机制让每个位置都能看到序列里的其他位置。查询、键和值都是同一个输入的
线性投影，注意力权重由查询和键的点积决定，再经过 softmax 归一化。这个结构没有
循环，也没有卷积，所以整个序列可以并行计算，这是 Transformer 能扩展到今天规模
的根本原因。

多头注意力把表示空间切成若干子空间，每个头独立计算注意力，然后拼接回去。不同的
头会学到不同的关系：有的关注相邻词，有的关注句法依存，有的负责把主语和很远的
谓语连起来。单头也能工作，但表达能力会明显下降。

位置编码解决的是注意力本身不区分顺序的问题。绝对位置编码把位置信息加进输入，
相对位置编码把它加进注意力分数，旋转位置编码则把它变成对查询和键的旋转。
后者在长文本外推上表现更好，也是现在多数开源模型的默认选择。

Training a large model is mostly a systems problem. The mathematics fits on one
page; the difficulty is keeping tens of thousands of accelerators busy while
gradients, parameters and optimizer states move between them. Data parallelism,
tensor parallelism and pipeline parallelism each trade communication volume
against memory, and real systems combine all three.

Mixed precision training keeps a master copy of the weights in higher precision
while doing the forward and backward passes in lower precision. The loss scale
exists because gradients in low precision underflow to zero; multiplying the
loss before the backward pass shifts them into representable range, and the
optimizer divides it back out before the update.

推理和训练的瓶颈完全不同。训练是计算密集的，推理在自回归解码阶段是访存密集的：
每生成一个 token 都要把整个模型的参数从显存读一遍，而实际计算量只有一次矩阵
向量乘。这就是为什么批处理能极大提升吞吐——参数只读一次，却服务了很多请求。

KV 缓存把已经算过的键和值存下来，避免每步重算整个前缀。代价是显存占用随序列
长度线性增长，长上下文场景下缓存可能比模型权重本身还大。分组查询注意力和多查询
注意力就是为了压缩这部分开销。

Retrieval augmented generation does not make a model smarter. It changes what
information is in the context window when the model answers. The quality of the
answer is then bounded by the quality of the retrieval, which is why evaluating
a RAG system means evaluating retrieval and generation separately before
evaluating them together.

Evaluation is where most projects are least rigorous. A benchmark score without
the task definition, the prompt, the decoding parameters and the contamination
analysis is not a measurement. Two papers reporting different numbers on the
same benchmark are usually measuring different things.

对齐的目标是让模型的输出符合人的意图和价值判断。监督微调提供了正确行为的示例，
偏好优化则告诉模型在两个都合理的回答之间应该更倾向哪一个。前者教会格式和风格，
后者调整的是更细的取舍。

护栏不能只放在模型这一层。输入侧要处理注入和越权，输出侧要检查敏感信息和事实性，
工具调用侧要限制权限和副作用。任何一层缺失，整个系统的安全性就等于最弱的那一环。

An agent is a loop, not a model. The model chooses an action, the environment
executes it and returns an observation, and the loop repeats until a stopping
condition. Reliability comes from making that loop observable, interruptible and
verifiable — not from asking the model to think harder.

Cost in production is dominated by tokens, and tokens are dominated by context.
Caching prompts, trimming retrieved passages and choosing a smaller model for
routing decisions usually saves more than any single optimization inside the
serving stack.

监控大模型系统需要记录完整轨迹：用户输入、检索到的片段、模型输出、工具调用和
最终结果。只记录请求延迟和错误率是不够的，因为质量下降往往不表现为报错，而是
表现为回答变得平庸或开始编造。

Scaling laws describe how loss falls as a power law in model size, data size and
compute. They are empirical regularities, not theorems, and they hold over the
range where they were measured. Their practical value is in allocating a fixed
compute budget between more parameters and more data.

The most common failure in applied work is not a wrong model choice. It is a
missing feedback loop: no one is measuring whether the system got better after
the last change, so every change is a guess.
